Technology has changed the way people live, work and communicate. A few decades ago, letters took days to arrive, and a simple question could take weeks to answer. Today a message crosses the planet in a second.
Still, many people say that real learning needs time and patience. A student who reads a book slowly often remembers more than someone who only looks at a screen for a minute.
The best solution is to use both: modern tools to find information quickly, and quiet hours to think about it carefully.
